How this works

StepWhat happensWhat the visitor sees
1 · SubmitThe browser validates the email and requires all three answers.An inline message naming what is missing; focus moves to it.
2 · WriteThe record is written to IndexedDB with a UUID and an ISO timestamp.“Writing the record…” and the submit button is disabled.
3 · ConfirmOnly after the write resolves is the record shown as stored.The recommendation card plus a receipt with the reference and timestamp.
4 · RelayOptional POST to meta.captureEndpoint in content.json.“Recorded and sent.” or “Saved, but not relayed” with the endpoint's error.
5 · FailA blocked or full store throws; the record is not claimed.“Could not save that” with the reason and advice; an error row is logged below.
